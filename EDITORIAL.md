# Publishing instructions

Owner: Anzolo Kpazhi Hope. Publish one useful original article per daily scheduled run. This is the owner's authorized personal journal, not a peer-reviewed academic journal.

Write in first person, speaking directly to one reader. Begin with a relatable African business, career or engineering problem. Explain the mechanism, give concrete steps, discuss realistic limits, and finish with one manageable action. Connect to ECHUKU, KONTA, DZEVA or the fellowship only where relevant. Distinguish proposed design from released functionality. Do not invent client results, personal experiences, qualifications, statistics, endorsements, quotes or ranking scores. Use fresh authoritative references for changeable or empirical claims. Do not recycle a post merely to meet frequency.

Fetch current main before editing. Review existing article titles to avoid duplication. Add one content/<slug>.json using an existing article's schema: slug, title, category, excerpt, date (actual Lagos date), author, sections (heading, paragraphs), takeaway, media. `media` is a list of IDs in content/media.json; use only genuinely related entries. Preserve all existing content. Build with scripts/build.py; validate with scripts/check.py. Commit source and regenerated dist in one atomic commit against the latest main with an expected-head check. Do not overwrite concurrent updates.

The shared template includes the verified Google Form stored in content/site.json below every article. Keep the guide free and use the community name Anzolo Hope AI Fellowship. Do not claim the form automatically enrolls a member: delivery/enrolment behaviour has not been tested. Do not submit someone else's details to test it.

Check deployment and the resulting URL. Report the article link only as live after verification. If Pages or access is blocked, preserve the work and report the specific blocker. Never purchase hosting, subscriptions, API credits or promotions. Do not email or message third parties.

For press, thank the publication and describe what that source actually reports; then make an explicitly present-day connection to work. Do not imply historical reporting endorses later products. PositiveNaija is sponsored. DEV is community commentary. Duskriplez's score is that site's editorial opinion, not a search-engine/industry ranking. Vanguard has a restrictive excerpt allowance: retain its title and link without expanding its source summary.

Never publish private client operations, account credentials, personnel records or unpublished manuscripts. New case studies require confirmed public facts or owner-provided publishable information. Prefer a hypothetical example clearly introduced as one.
